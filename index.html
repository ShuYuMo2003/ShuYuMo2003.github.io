GPT 6 Astra as an Embodied Policy
Jiayi Su*, Yixin Zheng*, Mi Yan, Li Yi, Zhizheng Zhang✉, He Wang✉
Technical Report, 2026
project page / code / 具身智能之心 / 腾讯科技 / 智能纪元AGI / human five

A technical report comparing direct GPT 6 Astra control with a hybrid π0.5 + GPT 6 Astra policy for zero-shot bimanual manipulation. On the selected RoboDojo tasks, the hybrid policy reaches 48% success and a 62.60 mean score, compared with 26% and 37.81 for direct control.
